### Your numbers (from the step 2 math)

- **Break-even ad cost per sale: $29.65.** Above this, every sale loses money.
- **Target ad cost per sale: $11.65 or less.** That's what it takes to hit 30% net at the $59.99 price.
- **Realistic first-month ad cost per sale: about $16–25.** Expect to sit between break-even and target at first. That's normal while testing.

### Timing warning: November and December

Today is November 2, 2026, so ads would start right before Black Friday and Cyber Monday (Nov 27–30). Ad prices on Meta and TikTok can be 30–80% higher than usual from mid-November through December, because big retailers outbid everyone. On the plus side, this is a giftable product, and shipping from a US warehouse can still beat Christmas deadlines.

The plan below starts small. If week 3 numbers are bad, pause and relaunch the first week of January, when "new year, new habits" wellness demand rises and ad prices drop.

### Week 1 (Nov 2–8): Set up (~10 hrs)

- [ ] Pick the name, then register the domain and social handles
- [ ] Open Shopify, choose a free theme (Dawn or Sense), and connect the domain
- [ ] Install the CJdropshipping app, import the product, and **confirm it's stocked in a US warehouse**
- [ ] **Place the sample order** to your own address
- [ ] Paste in the store content and fill every `[bracket]`
- [ ] Set up Shopify Payments, taxes (Shopify Tax), and a support email on your domain
- [ ] Install the Meta pixel and Conversions API through Shopify's Facebook & Instagram app
- [ ] Place a test order with Shopify's Bogus Gateway (test mode) to check that checkout works

### Week 2 (Nov 9–15): Make content and test organic posting (~10 hrs)

When the sample arrives:
- [ ] Film **10–15 short vertical videos** on your phone:
  - unboxing
  - "first 60 seconds on the mat" (your honest reaction)
  - evening routine
  - "close-up of the spikes"
  - phone-free wind-down
- [ ] Take photos for the product page: lifestyle shots in your own space, plus a flat lay
- [ ] Post 1–2 videos a day on TikTok and Instagram Reels. **This is free testing.** Videos that hold attention organically are your best ad candidates.
- [ ] Check real delivery time against what you promise. Adjust the shipping policy if it's off.

### Week 3 (Nov 16–22): Start paid ads (~8 hrs)

- [ ] **Meta (Facebook + Instagram)**: one Advantage+ Sales campaign at **$20–25/day**, US only, with the 3–5 best organic videos as ads
- [ ] Keep posting organically every day
- [ ] Optional: boost a TikTok post with Spark Ads ($10–15/day) only if a video is already doing well organically. Don't run TikTok and Meta ads together on a small budget.
- [ ] **When to stop an ad:**
  - It has spent **$30 (about one break-even sale) with no add-to-carts** → turn it off
  - It has spent **$60 with no purchases** → turn it off
  - Its ad cost per sale is under $25 after 3+ sales → keep it running
- [ ] Don't edit ads more than once every 48 hours. Meta's algorithm needs time to learn.

### Week 4 (Nov 23–Dec 2): Black Friday, then decide (~8 hrs)

- [ ] Optional Black Friday offer: **"Buy 2, save 20%"** (gift angle). A bundle protects margin better than a sitewide discount.
- [ ] Watch ad prices daily over Black Friday weekend. If ad cost per sale goes above $29.65, cut spend in half.
- [ ] **Dec 1–2: go / no-go review** using the metrics below

### Metrics to watch

| Metric | Healthy | Warning sign | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|---|
| Link click-through rate (Meta) | above 1.5% | under 0.8% | Whether the ad *creative* works |
| Cost per link click | under $1.50 | over $2.50 | Ad competition plus creative quality |
| Add-to-cart rate | above 6% of sessions | under 3% | Whether the *product page and price* work |
| Conversion rate | 1.5–3% of sessions | under 1% | Trust, shipping info and checkout |
| **Ad cost per sale** | **under $16, aiming for $11.65** | **over $29.65** | **The one number that decides profit** |
| Average order value | above $65 (upsells working) | stuck at $59.99 | Your main lever to reach 30% net |
| Refund + chargeback rate | under 5% | over 10% | Product quality or delivery problems |
| Delivery time (actual) | 3–7 business days | over 10 | Supplier problems. Fix this before scaling. |

### Day-30 decision

- **Scale** if ad cost per sale is under $16 and refunds are under 5%. Raise the budget 20% every 3 days, add the wake-up light as a second product, and start collecting emails with a 10% off popup.
- **Fix and retest in January** if people click but don't buy (good click-through rate, few sales). Test a new price, the offer, or the product page, not more ad spend.
- **Kill the product** if click-through rate stays under 0.8% across 10+ different videos, or ad cost per sale stays above break-even. Move to product #2 from the shortlist and reuse the store and brand.

### Realistic expectations

- Most first products don't make a profit in month one. A good month one is **breaking even with data**: you know which videos, audiences and offers work.
- Plan on losing up to your ad test budget ($350–650). Don't spend money you'd need back.
- Your best long-term edge is your own filmed content and a real brand, not the product. The same mat sells on Amazon for $25–40.
