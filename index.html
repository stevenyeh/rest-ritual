Questions

Does it hurt?

The first few minutes feel prickly, and that's normal. Start on a thin shirt or a bed, then move to bare skin when you're ready.

How long should I use it?

Start with 5–10 minutes and work up to 20.

Who shouldn't use it?

Don't use the mat if you're pregnant, have a skin condition, a bleeding disorder or a heart condition, or are diabetic with reduced sensation, unless your doctor says it's OK. If you're unsure, ask your doctor first.
