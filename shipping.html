Shipping Policy

Where we ship

United States (all 50 states). Alaska, Hawaii and PO boxes TBD

Cost

Free standard shipping on every order.

Processing time

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days (Monday–Friday, excluding US holidays).

Delivery time

After processing, standard delivery takes 3–7 business days. You'll get a tracking number by email once your order ships.

Holiday orders

For delivery before December 25, order by date TBD. Carriers slow down in December, and we can't guarantee delivery dates.

Lost or delayed packages

If your tracking hasn't updated in 7 days, or your order hasn't arrived within 14 business days, email us at support email TBD and we'll send a replacement or a refund.

Wrong address

Please check your address at checkout. If you contact us within 12 hours we can usually fix it before the order ships.
