Returns & Refunds

30-day returns

If you're not happy, you can return your set within 30 days of delivery for a refund.

Condition

Items must be unused, with the cover clean, and returned in the original packaging. For hygiene reasons we can't accept mats that have been used, unless they arrived damaged or defective.

Damaged or defective items

Email us within 7 days of delivery at support email TBD with a photo. We'll send a replacement or a full refund. You won't need to return the item.

How to return

Email us with your order number. We'll reply within 1 business day with the return address. Please don't send items back without contacting us first.

Return shipping

The customer pays return shipping for change-of-mind returns. We pay for damaged, defective or incorrect items.

Refunds

Refunds go to your original payment method within 5 business days of the return arriving. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.
